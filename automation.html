AI Automation

The work that runs without you.

Every business has a list of jobs that eat the day and grow nothing: chasing the same enquiry twice, retyping an invoice, copying numbers into a spreadsheet on a Thursday night. We build systems that do those jobs quietly in the background, so your team spends its hours on the work only people can do.

What we automate

Start with the job you
most dread doing.

Most businesses we meet want one of these four. You do not have to know how it should work. That is the part we figure out.

Lead capture and follow up

An enquiry arrives from your site, your NFC card or Instagram. It gets logged, sorted by how serious it looks, and answered on WhatsApp within seconds rather than whenever somebody next checks the inbox. Nothing sits unread over a weekend.

  • Instant replies
  • Lead scoring
  • CRM logging
  • Reminders to chase
  • WhatsApp and email

Customer conversations

An AI agent trained on your actual business: your prices, your hours, your services, your way of speaking. It handles the questions you answer fifty times a week and hands over to a human the moment something needs one. It never guesses.

  • WhatsApp agents
  • Website chat
  • Booking and enquiries
  • Arabic and English
  • Human handover
02

Documents and data entry

Quotes and invoices generated and sent without anyone opening a template. Receipts and supplier bills read automatically and filed where your accountant expects them. The typing job that nobody wants, gone.

  • Quotes and invoices
  • Receipt reading
  • Contract summaries
  • Spreadsheet syncing
  • Accounting handoff

Reporting and internal admin

Your numbers pulled from wherever they live and assembled into one report that arrives every Monday, already written. Staff onboarding, scheduling and stock alerts that trigger themselves instead of relying on somebody remembering.

How we build it

We watch the work first.

  1. 01

    Map

    We sit with whoever actually does the task and follow it step by step. Half the time the fix is simpler than the software anyone tried to sell you.

  2. 02

    Build

    We connect the tools you already pay for rather than moving you onto something new. You see it working on real cases before it touches a live customer.

  3. 03

    Watch

    Automations break when the world around them changes. We monitor yours, fix what drifts, and tell you plainly when a job is better left to a person.
