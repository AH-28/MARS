Smart NFC

One tap, and your brand does the talking.

Paper cards get thrown away. A MARS card gets tapped, and in one second your contact details, your links, your booking page or your Google review form are open on their phone. No app to download. No typing. Nothing to lose in a drawer.

How it works

Three seconds, start to finish.

  1. 01

    They tap

    Your card, stand or tag touches the back of their phone. Every iPhone since the XR and virtually every modern Android reads it, with no app, either side.

  2. 02

    Your page opens

    A page built in your brand, not a template with someone else's logo on it. Live in a second, on a link you own and we can change any time.

  3. 03

    They act

    Save your contact, message you on WhatsApp, follow you, book you, or leave the review. One tap from a handshake to something that actually counts.
